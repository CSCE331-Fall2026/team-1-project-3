## Retrospective
11/22/26
**Attendance:** Pablo, Samuel, Ashley, Sharaf, Colby

**What went well?**
Version control has been down to a science, with everyone fully understanding what to do, making for an efficient workflow. 
We also had smaller and more managable tasks which led to a better utilization of our sprint board. 
Lastly, we did pretty well on the communication, everyone seems to stay up to date with what was going on.

**What did not go well?**
We fell behind on pull requests and tasks towards the end of the sprint, causing a bit of a rush to complete everything on time.
We also were a bit slow on merging pull requests, which caused a few delays in the workflow process.

**How many story points were completed vs planned?**
Unfortunately we had 15 points that we did not complete. 
We seem to have underestimated the difficulty of fixing our bugs as well as implementing a new view at the same time.
