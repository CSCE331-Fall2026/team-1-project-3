# User Study 1

## Study Goals
- Is the design intuitive to use, can a user understand how to navigate the menus without having helped make them?
- Is there anything that holds up the user and/or blocks them from smoothly completing an order?
- Are the steps taken to complete any task unnecessary or require more complicated actions than needed?

## Questions Asked

### Likert-scale
1. User can successfully complete their tasks - Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree 
2. The design is intuitive - Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree 
3. The design was simple and easy to use - Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree 
4. The information that was given was easy to understand - Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree 

### Open-ended / interview
1. Was there any information that you could not understand, even slightly? If so, what?
2. Please state any other issues or improvements.

### Participant B – Manager (session run by: Ashley Hoang)
- Likert ratings: Strongly Agree, Agree, Strongly Agree, Agree
- Notable quotes: “What is ‘unit size’ in menu item?”
- Observed behavior (hesitations, errors, friction): hesitated during inputting a new item in menu due to confusion of certain details, a bit of confusion with what “change stock” meant, inputting different time periods in graphs is not easy

## Proposed Design Moves (prioritized)
| Priority | Proposed change | Evidence / justification | Sprint impact |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| 8 | Change time period input to a calendar selector | User took too much effort to manually write the time period and it was easy to make a typo | Medium impact to the sprint as the functions to change time period will have to be completely changed |
| 1 | Add clarification in adding menu items | User was confused on what “unit size” was | Not much impact on the sprint as it is a quick and easy change |
| 1 | Rename “Change Stock” to “Add/Subtract Stock” | User thought that “change stock” meant to completely overwrite it | Not much impact on the sprint as it is a quick and easy change |

### Participant C – Cashier (session run by: Colby Ideker)
- Likert ratings: Agree, Agree, Agree, Neutral
- Notable quotes: “How do I undo it?”
- Observed behavior (hesitations, errors, friction): Wasn’t sure what the checkboxes for the menu items were supposed to do, didn't expect it to fully remove the item.

## Proposed Design Moves (prioritized)
| Priority | Proposed change | Evidence / justification | Sprint impact |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Replace cart checkbox with an X or a clear remove button | User thought the checkbox would select the item or let you edit it | |
| 2 | Show Prices / upcharges directly in the menu | The user wasn’t sure why some items were more expensive than others and believed it would be important for the cashier to quickly know that information to tell customers. | |
| 3 | Show a progress counter and allow users to “undo” selections for meal items | The user thinks it would be helpful to be able to know where you are at in the selection in case you lose focus / are new to the menu, and allow you to undo mistakes without remaking the item. | |

### Participant D – Customer (session run by: Samuel Just)
- Likert ratings: Agree, Strongly Agree, Agree, Strongly Agree
- Notable quotes: Issues with figuring out how many items are in each (“How many items do I need for a plate?”), seasonal items are showing even outside of the seasons (“Why can’t I buy the Orange Chicken?”).
- Observed behavior (hesitations, errors, friction): Lots of errors found, causing confusion. They were able to figure out how to move for the most part.

## Proposed Design Moves (prioritized)
| Priority | Proposed change | Evidence / justification | Sprint impact |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| 2 | Fix button selection bugs with the customer interface | The user could not unselect items | Low |
| 1 | Make the meal items more clear in how many entrees and sides they expect | The user had trouble understanding what meals were worth | Medium |
| 2 | Fix bug where seasonal items showed up even outside of their intended season | The user tried to order an out-of-season seasonal item and everything broke | Medium |

### Participant E – Customer (session run by: Sharaf Mehmood)
- Likert ratings: Agree, Agree, Strongly Agree, Strongly Agree
- Notable quotes: Would want to see more details about the items (such calorie count and a description).
- Observed behavior (hesitations, errors, friction): No notable observations, was able to perform an order fine.


## Proposed Design Moves (prioritized)
| Priority | Proposed change | Evidence / justification | Sprint impact |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Add a way to see more information about items (like show relevant information when clicking on it) | User had issues trying to understand the items and their information (like calories) | Medium |
