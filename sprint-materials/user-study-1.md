# User Study 1

## Study Goals
- Figure out if the design is intuitive to use.
- Figure out if there are any unknown bugs.
- Figure out if there are any frustrations that could arise from the design.
- Figure out if the information provided by the website is easy to understand.

## Questions Asked
### Likert-scale
1. User can successfully complete their tasks. — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree
2. The design is intuitive. — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree
3. The design was simple and easy to use. — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree
4. The information that was given was easy to understand . — Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree


### Open-ended / interview
1. Was there any information that you could not understand, even slightly? If so, what?
2. Please state any other issues or improvements.

## Proposed Design Moves (prioritized)
| Priority | Proposed change | Evidence / justification | Sprint impact |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| 8 |  Change time period input to a calendar selector | User took too much effort to manually write the time period and it was easy to make a typo | Medium impact to the sprint as the functions to change time period will have to be completely changed |
| 1 |  Add clarification in adding menu items | User was confused on what “unit size” was | Not much impact on the sprint as it is a quick and easy change |
| 1 |  Rename “Change Stock” to “Add/Subtract Stock” | User thought that “change stock” meant to completely overwrite it | Not much impact on the sprint as it is a quick and easy change |
